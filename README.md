
# MapleTrust Financial Services | Data Loss Prevention (DLP) Policy
**Policy ID:** MTFS-DLP-001  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Draft  
**Policy Owner:** Information Security Team  
**Review Frequency:** Annually  
---

### 1. Purpose

This policy establishes requirements to protect MapleTrust Financial Services' sensitive information from unauthorized disclosure, transfer, or loss.  The policy aims to reduce the risk of data breaches, protect customer financial information, and support compliance with applicable data protection requirements.  
### 2. Scope
This policy applies to all MapleTrust employees, contractors, and third parties who access, process, store, or transmit company information.  It covers sensitive information held in company systems, email, cloud applications, endpoints, and approved third party environments.

### 3. Safeguards

To protect sensitive information, MapleTrust Financial Services shall implement the following safeguards:

**DLP-01 — Data Inventory**

Maintain an inventory of sensitive information, including its location, classification, and assigned data owner.

**DLP-02 — Data Classification**

Classify information according to its sensitivity and business value. Customer financial records, personally identifiable information (PII), and payment card information must be protected according to their classification.

**DLP-03 — Data Loss Prevention Controls**

Implement DLP controls across applicable email, endpoints, and cloud applications to detect and prevent unauthorized disclosure or transfer of sensitive information.

**DLP-04 — Data Transfer Protection**

Restrict unauthorized sharing of sensitive information through email, removable storage, cloud services, and other data transfer channels.

**DLP-05 — Monitoring and Alerts**

Monitor DLP events, investigate suspected data leakage, and escalate confirmed incidents to the Information Security Team.

**DLP-06 — Access Control**

Restrict access to sensitive information based on business needs and the principle of least privilege.

**DLP-07 — Data Encryption**

Protect sensitive information through approved encryption methods during storage and transmission.

**DLP-08 — Control Testing**

Periodically test DLP controls to confirm they are operating as intended. Document control failures and track corrective actions through closure.

**DLP-09 — Policy Exceptions**

Document and assess any exceptions to this policy. Exceptions must be approved by authorized management and reviewed periodically.

### 4. Roles and Responsibilities

**Information Security Team:** Oversees DLP controls, investigates alerts, and monitors compliance.

**IT Operations:** Implements and maintains DLP technical controls.

**Data Owners:** Identify, classify, and approve appropriate use of sensitive information.

**Employees and Contractors:** Follow data handling requirements and report suspected data loss incidents.

### 5. Policy Sanctions

Failure to comply with this policy may result in corrective or disciplinary action in accordance with MapleTrust's internal procedures.

Serious violations may result in termination of employment or contractual agreements, subject to applicable laws.

### 6. Policy Review

This policy shall be reviewed annually or following significant changes to business operations, technology, or regulatory requirements.
